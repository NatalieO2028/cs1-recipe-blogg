Soy Sauce Butter Noodles

Description

These soy sauce buttered noodles add a little bit of flavor to regular buttered noodles to spice them up!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 pounds of pasta of your choice
  • 3/4 quart chicken stock
  • parmesan cheese (as much as you'd like)
  • 1/2 stick of butter
  • salt and pepper
  • splash of soy sauce

Steps

  1. In a large pot, melt 2 tablespoons of butter
  2. Add your uncooked pasta and mix until it's all fully coated with butter
  3. Pour in chicken stock and let simmer until al dente
  4. Now on low heat add your salt and pepper, and as much soy sauce and parmesan cheese as you would like!
  5. Enjoy!
