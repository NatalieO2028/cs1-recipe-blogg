Soy Sauce Butter Noodles

Description

These soy sauce buttered noodles add a little bit of flavor to regular buttered noodles to spice them up!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 pounds of pasta of your choice
  • 3/4 quart chicken stock
  • parmesan cheese (as much as you'd like)
  • 1/2 stick of butter
  • salt and pepper
  • splash of soy sauce

Steps

  1. In a large pot, melt 2 tablespoons of butter
  2. Add your uncooked pasta and mix until it's all fully coated with butter
  3. Pour in chicken stock and let simmer until al dente
  4. Now on low heat add your salt and pepper, and as much soy sauce and parmesan cheese as you would like!
  5. Enjoy!

Potato Latkes

Description

These potato latkes are perfectly crispy, and have the familiar homey feeling to them, as a classic Jewish comfort food.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 2 large russet potatoes, peeled
  • 1/4 cup all purpose flour
  • 1/2 medium onion, peeled
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp kosher sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 large egg
  • vegetable oil, or extra light olive oil for frying
  • apple sauce to eat it with

Steps

  1. Grate the potatoes and onions on the large holes of a grater. Transfer the mixture to a cheesecloth or lint free dishtowel, and squeeze out as much of the liquid as possible.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together 1/4 cup flour, 1 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper, then whisk in the egg. Add the squeezed, dried potatoes and stir until they're evenly coated in the batter.
  3. Heat a large, heavy-bottomed skillet over medium-high heat and add about 1/4” of oil (enough to coat the bottom of the pan generously). Once the oil shimmers, add the batter to the pan 1 heaping tablespoon at a time (or use a trigger-release ice cream scoop) and immediately press with a spatula to form disks. Fry until the edges are browned, 4-5 minutes. Flip and cook until the second side is golden brown, about 3 to 4 minutes more.
  4. Transfer latkes to a paper-towel-lined plate and immediately sprinkle lightly with kosher salt. Repeat with the remaining potato mixture adding more oil to the skillet as needed. Serve warm latke with apple sauce.
  5. Enjoy!

Broccili cheddar mac and chese

Description

This pasta is a mix of a classic broccoli cheddar soup, and mac and cheese in one!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb small pasta shells
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 cup diced onion
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 5oz cottage cheese (one small carton)
  • 1 cup cheddar cheese, grated
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese, grated

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and cook pasta until al dente
  2. Add olive oil to large sauté pan and cook onion until translucent. add garlic and broccoli and cook until softened
  3. Add chicken broth and simmer 5-10 minutes until reduced a bit. reduce heat.
  4. In a small blender or magic bullet, blend cottage cheese with cream until smooth
  5. Add cottage cheese mixture to sauté pan, and stir to combine. slowly add the cheddar and mozzarella; stirring until a thick cheese sauce forms
  6. Add pasta and toss to coat. serve in bowls topped with more mozzarella
  7. Enjoy!
